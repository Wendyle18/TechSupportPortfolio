Evidence-led workflow

How I troubleshoot a technical support issue

  1. ReproduceConfirm the customer journey, affected surface, and exact behavior.
  2. Gather contextRecord the browser, store, theme, app, API, account, configuration, and environment details that matter.
  3. IsolateCompare working and failing states and narrow the issue to the best-supported layer.
  4. Resolve or work aroundProvide a practical next step when support can act safely and explain its limits.
  5. Escalate with evidenceShare scope, reproduction, expected and actual results, completed tests, and technical findings.
  6. DocumentTurn reusable findings into checklists, notes, templates, or knowledge material.
