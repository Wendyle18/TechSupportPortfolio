How I approach an issue

The workflow changes with the product, but the investigation remains evidence-led and understandable to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

  1. ReproduceConfirm the customer journey, affected surface, and exact behavior.
  2. Gather contextCollect browser, store, theme, app, API, configuration, and environment details.
  3. IsolateCompare working and failing states and narrow the problem to the most likely layer.
  4. Resolve or work aroundProvide a practical, clearly explained next step when support can act safely.
  5. Escalate with evidenceShare steps, scope, expected and actual results, and relevant technical findings.
  6. DocumentTurn repeatable findings into checklists, support notes, templates, or knowledge material.
