Privacy and analytics

This page explains the limited measurement used on this portfolio and the external services visitors may choose to open.

Google Analytics 4

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) with measurement ID G-58984RH5TE to understand how the portfolio is discovered and which pages and contact actions are useful.

GA4 may process information such as page views, broad device and browser details, approximate location derived from network information, referral context, and interactions with selected links. Google Analytics may use browser storage or similar technologies as part of that measurement. This portfolio does not send names, email addresses, résumé URLs with sensitive parameters, message contents, or other intentionally collected personal details in its custom analytics events.

Measured portfolio actions

The site records named events when a visitor chooses an email, hire, LinkedIn, GitHub, résumé-download, or project-demo link. These events identify the type and general page location of the action, not the visitor’s contact details or the contents of any communication.

Contact and forms

This website does not contain an account system, checkout, newsletter form, or database-backed contact form. Email links open the visitor’s chosen email application. Any message sent by email is handled by the visitor’s email provider and the receiving email service, not by this static website.

External services

The portfolio links to Google Drive, LinkedIn, GitHub, Shopify demo pages, and a public support workflow demo. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices when a visitor opens them.

Your browser choices

Browser privacy controls, content blockers, and analytics-blocking extensions may limit GA4. The portfolio’s pages, case studies, contact links, and résumé link continue to work when analytics is unavailable.
