How I Work

A support approach built for clear handoffs

I move from customer context to reproducible evidence, then communicate the next step so customers, support teams, and engineers can act with confidence.

01

Reproduce the issue

Confirm the reported behavior and identify the exact customer journey, product surface, or storefront state involved.

02

Gather the right context

Collect browser, account, theme, app, API, configuration, and environment details that can change the outcome.

03

Isolate the root cause

Compare working and failing states, test integrations and front-end behavior, and narrow the issue to a specific layer.

04

Resolve or provide a workaround

Explain the practical next step in plain language, including any limitations or follow-up checks the customer should expect.

05

Escalate with evidence

When engineering input is needed, provide reproducible steps, observed results, expected behavior, and relevant technical context.

06

Document recurring solutions

Turn repeatable findings into support notes, troubleshooting guides, reply templates, or knowledge-base material.
